High-Altitude Physiology

When traveling above 10,000 feet in Spiti and Zanskar, atmospheric pressure drops significantly. All guests undergo a mandatory 48-hour gentle rest interval upon landing in Leh or Kaza. Daily pulse oximeter screenings, hyperbaric oxygen chambers on standby, and structured hydration targets (4-5 liters of electrolyte-rich fluids daily) guarantee safe acclimatization before high pass ascents.

Zero-Footprint Wilderness Code

We operate under strict zero single-use plastic mandates across all wilderness accommodations. Bio-composting toilets, solar thermal heating, micro-hydel hydro-generators, and closed-loop greywater purification ensure that not a single gram of pollutant enters pristine river catchments or fragile alpine meadows. All organic kitchen waste is composted for local community orchards.

Indigenous Tribal Partnership

85% of expedition fees remain within local host communities. We employ indigenous Khasi, Ladakhi, Spitian, and Munnar hill tribe guides exclusively, providing fair-wage contracts, comprehensive mountaineering medical coverage, and seasonal guide academy certifications. In addition, 5% of all booking revenues fund village solar micro-grids and monastery preservation trusts.

Official Field Dossier & Expedition FAQ

The Comprehensive Traveler Handbook

01 How do flights operate into high-altitude and monsoon airfields?

Regional flight connections into Leh (IXL), Kullu Bhuntar (KUU), and Shillong (SHL) operate under Visual Flight Rules (VFR) due to mountainous terrain and distinct microclimates. Flights to Leh and Kullu are scheduled predominantly during early morning hours (06:00 to 10:30 AM) when thermal air turbulence and cross-valley winds are minimal. Modern ATR 72-600 and Airbus A320neo aircraft used by our partner carriers are fitted with specialized high-thrust engines and certified GPS Required Navigation Performance (RNP) receivers to maintain safe obstacle clearance margins through steep gorges.

02 What Inner Line Permits (ILP) and clearances are required?

Domestic and foreign visitors entering border frontiers in Spiti Valley (beyond Kaza towards Sumdo), Ladakh (Nubra Valley, Pangong Tso, and Zanskar), and border sectors of Meghalaya require valid Inner Line Permits issued by District Magistrates or the Ministry of Home Affairs. When booking your flights and expeditions through Flights.com, our specialized concierge automatically compiles your passport details, visa credentials, and photo identification to secure all protected area clearances 72 hours prior to arrival, delivering digital and physical stamped permits directly to your hotel upon landing.

03 What gear and physical conditioning are recommended for the Chadar and Spiti trails?

The Chadar Ice Trek in Zanskar and winter high-altitude excursions in Spiti demand specialized cold-weather protection capable of withstanding temperatures from -15°C to -35°C. We provide an authenticated 3-layer system: Merino wool thermal underwear, 800-fill goose down expedition parkas, and breathable three-layer GORE-TEX Pro outer shells. For footwear, gumboots with insulating neoprene insoles or rigid crampon-compatible mountaineering boots are essential. Prior cardio endurance conditioning (running, swimming, or brisk stair climbing for 4-6 weeks before departure) is strongly advised.

04 How does Flights.com ensure carbon neutrality and local community benefits?

We adhere to a verified 120% carbon-negative charter audited under UNFCCC Gold Standard criteria. For every metric ton of aviation fuel combusted on regional routes, we fund local conservation initiatives: high-altitude deodar pine reforestation in Himachal Pradesh, micro-hydel power installations in Zanskari hamlets, and conservation sanctuaries in the Western Ghats. Over 85% of accommodation and excursion revenues go directly to native community custodians, funding village schools, solar energy cells, and monastic preservation funds.

05 What baggage allowances and drone regulations apply in mountain regions?

Due to takeoff weight limitations at high-altitude airfields like Leh (IXL) and Kullu (KUU), regional turboprop flights enforce a strict check-in baggage limit of 15 kg per passenger, with cabin baggage restricted to 7 kg. Additional baggage can be pre-booked or stored securely at our transit lounges in New Delhi or Chandigarh. Please note that recreational drone flights are strictly prohibited near international border zones, military cantonments, and wildlife sanctuaries across Spiti, Ladakh, and Meghalaya without prior written clearance from the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA).
